ML: Chess 2.0


Home Rules Movement

Player 1: White always goes FIRST

1) Press START and state the tile of the piece you want to move then press CONFIRM
2) Press START again to state where the piece should move to then press CONFIRM

Player 2: Repeat Steps 1 and 2

Buttons: What Do They Do?

Start Button:

This Button Enables the Microphone

Stop Button:

This Button Disables the Microphone

Reset Button:

This Button Resets the Chess Board


General Rules

  • Player who plays the White Piece always goes first
  • Each player takes turns to make a single move and cannot skip turns
  • Each piece moves in a specific way and must be legal (Check "Movement under Info")
  • If Player's piece lands on an opponent’s piece, opponent's captured and removed from the board
  • Goal is to checkmate the opponent´s King
