Chess


Home Rules Movement

Pawn

Pawns move one square forward in a straight line and cannot move horizontally or backwards at any point of the game.
However, there are 2 exceptions:

1) At a pawn's inital location, they can move forward 2 spaces rather than 1

2)The Player can choose to move forward or diagonally when an opponent piece is diagonally forward of the pawn

King

The King is only allowed to move a single space horizontally, vertically or diagonally.The king cannot move to a space that would cause a check or checkmate

Queen

The Queen is an extremely versatile piece as it is able to move diagonally, horizontally, or vertically. The queen cannot move through pieces of the same color and captures a piece of the opposite colour by moving onto its square

Bishop

Bishops are able to move in any diagonal direction. Each Bishop can only move on the same color squares as it started the game

Knight

Knights are able to move in what is known as an "L” shape which means they can move:

1) 2 squares forward, backward, left or right, then 1 square horizontally or vertically

2) 1 square forward, backward, left or right, then 2 squares horizontally or vertically

Rook / Castle

Rooks are able to move to any location vertically or horizontally from it's location. It is also able to moved while castling*

Castling: When King moves two squares to the right or 3 squares to the left in which the Rook is able to jump over the King and land on the square next to it
